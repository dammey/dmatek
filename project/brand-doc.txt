1. V2.1 Changes
Parent foundation and visual DNA remain locked.
Business architecture is now a formal identity layer.
ONE D’MATEK. MANY SPECIALISTS. is the architecture principle.
WE STAY is elevated to a customer relationship principle.
Branded House with Selective Identity is established.
The six operating businesses are explicitly defined.
PROBLEM FIRST. TECHNOLOGY SECOND. IMPACT ALWAYS. is internal strategic territory.
Complexity belongs behind the brand. Clarity belongs in front of the customer.

3. Visual Identity
Human Progress: technology with humanity. Precision × Warmth is the governing visual tension. Green carries the brand; gold punctuates. Manrope provides clarity and flexibility. The D’Matek Line provides a proprietary connective behaviour.

7. How It Should Feel
A customer can start with D’Matek without knowing which division they need.
A multi-business project feels coordinated by D’Matek.
Specialist expertise adds confidence, not complexity.
Distinctive business identities emerge only where maturity and market need justify them.
The parent brand remains the connective trust layer.
8. Application Test

Does it reduce complexity for the customer?
Does it show what becomes possible because the problem was solved?
